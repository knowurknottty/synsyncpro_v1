# Protocol Plan System - User Guide

## What Are Protocol Plans?

Protocol Plans are structured recommendations from practitioners for using SynSync Pro's brainwave entrainment protocols. They provide:

- **Scheduled Sessions**: Automatically scheduled protocol sessions
- **Progress Tracking**: Track adherence, streaks, and milestones
- **Check-Ins**: Periodic questionnaires to monitor progress
- **Metrics**: Record mood, anxiety, and other subjective measures
- **Guidance**: Instructions before, during, and after sessions

**Important**: Protocol Plans are wellness recommendations, not medical prescriptions. They do not replace professional medical care.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Import a Protocol Plan

1. Obtain a plan JSON file from your practitioner
2. Click "Import Protocol Plan" in the app
3. Drag and drop the JSON file or click to browse
4. Review validation results (errors must be fixed, warnings can be ignored)

### 2. Review and Accept

1. Read practitioner information carefully
2. Review contraindications and safety notes
3. Check all acknowledgment boxes
4. Click "Accept Plan" to begin

### 3. Set Your Start Date

Choose when you want to begin the protocol. The calendar will automatically generate all scheduled sessions based on:
- Phase structure
- Session frequency (daily, 2x/day, 3x/week, etc.)
- Time of day preferences
- Rest days

## Using Protocol Plans

### Calendar View

The calendar shows all scheduled sessions:
- **Blue dots**: Upcoming sessions
- **Green checkmarks**: Completed sessions
- **Red X**: Missed sessions

Click any day to see session details and start sessions.

### Starting a Session

Sessions can only be started within their time window (typically ±30 minutes):

1. Click "Start Session" on the calendar or upcoming sessions widget
2. Complete pre-session metrics (if required)
3. Follow the during-session instructions
4. Complete post-session metrics
5. Add optional session notes

### Tracking Progress

Your progress dashboard shows:
- **Adherence Rate**: Percentage of completed sessions
- **Current Streak**: Consecutive days with sessions
- **Milestones**: Achievements unlocked
- **Phase Progress**: Current phase completion

### Check-Ins

Check-ins are scheduled questionnaires at specific points:
- You'll be notified when a check-in is due
- Answer all required questions
- Responses help you and your practitioner track progress

### Milestones

Milestones celebrate your progress:
- Based on days completed or session count
- Unlock automatically when achieved
- View reward messages and badges

## Managing Your Plan

### Pause/Resume

Life happens. You can pause your plan:
1. Click "Pause Plan" in the overview
2. Resume when ready
3. The schedule adjusts to account for pause duration

### Missed Sessions

If you miss a session:
- It will be marked as "missed" automatically
- You can manually mark it complete if you did it offline
- Check your plan's "if missed session" instructions

### Exporting Data

Export your progress data:
- **JSON**: Complete data backup
- **CSV**: Spreadsheet-friendly session log
- Choose date range and privacy options

## Best Practices

### 1. Consistency is Key
- Complete sessions on schedule when possible
- Build a routine around your session times
- Use notifications to remind you

### 2. Track Honestly
- Record metrics honestly, even if progress is slow
- Use session notes to capture observations
- Complete check-ins thoroughly

### 3. Communicate with Your Practitioner
- Export your data for appointments
- Discuss any difficulties or side effects
- Request plan adjustments if needed

### 4. Listen to Your Body
- Stop if you experience adverse effects
- Respect the recommended session spacing
- Don't rush through phases

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Start Session
- Check that you're within the time window (±30 min)
- Ensure your plan is not paused
- Verify the protocol exists in your app

### Missed Session Marked Wrong
- Use "Mark as Complete" to manually record completion
- Add a note explaining the circumstance

### Calendar Not Updating
- Check your timezone settings
- Refresh the calendar view
- Ensure the plan has a start date

### Notifications Not Working
- Check notification permissions in browser settings
- Enable notifications in plan settings
- Test notifications to verify they work

## Data Privacy

All Protocol Plan data is stored locally on your device:
- No data is sent to servers
- You control all exports and sharing
- Deleting a plan removes all associated data

---

## Safety Reminders

- **Headphones Required**: Binaural protocols need stereo headphones
- **Seizure Warning**: Do not use if you have epilepsy or seizure disorders
- **Medical Conditions**: Consult your doctor before starting
- **Stop if Needed**: Discontinue immediately if you experience adverse effects
